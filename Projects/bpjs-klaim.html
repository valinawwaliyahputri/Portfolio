✦ About the Project

Summary

As the final project for General Linear Model, this project applied binary logistic regression analysis to classify high-cost and normal insurance claim groups based on cancer diagnoses and patient data from BPJS FKRTL samples (2019–2020), using a generalized linear model.

✦ Tools & Stack

RPython

✦ Work Process

01

Collect & Prepare Data

Preparing patient and cancer diagnosis data from the 2019–2020 BPJS FKRTL samples.

02

Explore & Analyze

Exploring the data to identify predictor variables for claims.

03

Build Regression Model

Applying binary logistic regression to classify claim groups.

04

Deliver Findings

Summarizing classification results as claim risk analysis insights.
