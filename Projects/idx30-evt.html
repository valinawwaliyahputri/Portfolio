✦ About the Project

Summary

Carried out during an internship at Otoritas Jasa Keuangan, this project applied Extreme Value Theory (EVT) and multivariate control charts to detect anomalies in stock returns within the IDX30 portfolio. The approach demonstrated strong statistical modeling and risk analytics skills in the context of financial sector supervision.
