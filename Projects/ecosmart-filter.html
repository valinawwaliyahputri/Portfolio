✦ About the Project

Summary

Created for the National Creative Competition (NCC) #2 2025 organized by UNY, this project analyzed and visualized microplastic waste risk data through an infographic. The visual narrative was designed to communicate Ecosmart Filter as an innovative microplastic filtering solution to stakeholders, and won 3rd Honorable Mention in the competition's infographic category.
