✦ About the Project

Summary

As the final project for Structural Equation Model, this project applied Multigroup Structural Equation Modeling (SEM) to analyze the effects of education, economy, and health on the Human Development Index (HDI) through the mediating role of poverty across Indonesian provinces in 2019 and 2024.

✦ Tools & Stack

SmartPLSExcel

✦ Work Process

01

Collect & Prepare Data

Preparing education, economy, health, poverty, and HDI data per province.

02

Design SEM Model

Designing a multigroup structural model with poverty as the mediating variable.

03

Test & Analyze

Testing the model using SmartPLS and analyzing the effect pathways between variables.

04

Deliver Findings

Summarizing the effect findings between 2019 and 2024.
