✦ About the Project

Summary

Built for the National Innovation Competition (NIC) Nacoesta UMY 2025, this project analyzed comparative data on Indonesia's education quality against ASEAN countries. The findings were turned into a data-driven infographic positioning EdTech as a scalable, inclusive solution to bridge Indonesia's education gap.
