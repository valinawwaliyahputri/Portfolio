✳ About the Company

PT MRT Jakarta (Perseroda) is a Regional Government-Owned Enterprise (BUMD) 99.997% owned by the DKI Jakarta Provincial Government, founded on 17 June 2008 and commercially operating since 2019. The company is responsible for the construction, operation, and maintenance of Jakarta's Mass Rapid Transit (MRT) system, as well as developing digital products and property business around stations and depots — including ticketing services, e-money, and passenger loyalty programs.

✳ Job Description

  • Collected, validated, and analyzed behavioral and purchasing data from 250,000+ users to drive customer segmentation and consumer-behavior insights, informing campaign targeting and product strategy across multiple MRT digital products.
  • Formulated ticket-bundling price recommendations for the MRT–Telkomsel partnership and benchmarked MartiShop pricing against competitors (GoFood, ShopeeFood) to balance customer acceptance with competitive positioning.
  • Forecasted user growth and revenue for e-money payment methods and ran bimonthly performance reviews across ticketing payment channels and in-app loyalty points.
  • Developed automated BI dashboards and standardized 3.6M+ monthly transaction records into a formal SOP, creating a consistent data repository for accurate, ongoing performance monitoring.
  • Coordinated cross-team readiness and supported A/B testing for the rollout of four digital services and payment initiatives (Special Lane, MartiShop, Allride, and e-money card top-up).
